Privacy Policy

Blade Runners: Twisted System · Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Blade Runners: Twisted System (the "game") on iPhone, who collects it, and what choices you have. The game is published by yoGee studios ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (coins, skins, best scores, settings and the nickname you choose) is stored on your device only.
  • The game shows advertising supplied by Unity Ads. Unity collects some device information to deliver and measure those ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your nickname, coin balance, unlocked items, best scores, tutorial progress and settings in the app's local storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

The nickname shown in the game is generated at random or typed by you. Other runners you see in a match are computer-controlled, and your nickname is not shared with other players.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game displays rewarded videos, full-screen ads and banner ads provided by Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity may collect:

TypeExamples
Device identifiersApple's identifier for vendors (IDFV); the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you have allowed tracking for this app
Device and network informationDevice model, operating system version, language, time zone, connection type, IP address (which indicates an approximate location such as country or city)
Ad interaction dataWhich ads were shown, whether they were watched or tapped, and when
DiagnosticsInformation about errors in the ad software

Unity acts as an independent controller of this information. Its practices are described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Unity provides a data and privacy control inside its ads (the small information icon shown on an ad) where you can view its choices and request access to or deletion of data it holds.

The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Without that permission iOS does not make the advertising identifier available, and ads are not personalised using it.

In-app purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game that a purchase succeeded so the coins can be added. We do not receive your name, card number or billing address. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not operate our own servers for this game and do not build profiles of players.
  • We do not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset on-device data: delete the game from your iPhone.
  • Limit ad tracking: on your iPhone open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Access or delete ad data: use the privacy control inside a Unity ad, or the options described in Unity's privacy policy linked above.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about players ourselves, these requests are normally fulfilled by Unity or Apple; contact us if you need help directing a request.

Data retention and security

Data saved by the game stays on your device until you delete the game. Information collected by Unity or Apple is retained according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and its effective date.
