Hex Appeal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Hex Appeal is a puzzle game made by yoGee studios. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores

Your progress, stars, Sparks, hints, settings and unlocked items are saved only on your device. We do not run servers for the game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

Advertising

Hex Appeal shows ads through Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies. To deliver and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect information such as your device's identifier for vendors, device type and settings, approximate location derived from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Hex Appeal does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

You can remove interstitial ads with the Remove Ads or Starter Pack purchase.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Hex Appeal is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
