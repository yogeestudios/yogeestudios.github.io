Support

Need help with one of our games? Email yogeestudios@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Fliplits

How do I play? Tap a tile to wake it. Its light flips the next tile it points at. Light every tile with the sparks you have.

Found a bug or need help with a purchase? Email yogeestudios@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. Privacy policy.

Snap Bandits

How do I play? Swipe across a rubber band to snip it. Get every bandit into the tube that matches its color. Fewer snips earn more stars.

Restore purchases: open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom.

Found a bug or need help with a purchase? Email yogeestudios@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. Privacy policy.
