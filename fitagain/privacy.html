Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Applies to the FitAgain iOS app (version 2.0 and later)

The short version

  • We collect what we need to build your plan from your photos and answers, run your weekly check-ins, and keep your account and subscription working.
  • Your body photos and answers are sent to Anthropic (Claude), our AI provider, only to analyse them and write your plan. They are not used to train AI models.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • Apple Health is optional. If you turn it on, FitAgain saves your finished workouts and check-in weight to Health and reads your step count. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your device except as described here.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account).

1. Who we are

FitAgain is made by yoGee studios, an independent developer ("FitAgain", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: yogeestudios@gmail.com.

3. Health data

Your body measurements, photos and training are health and fitness data. We use them only to provide the app's features. We do not use them for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we do not sell or share them except with the service providers in section 4, who process them for us.

Apple Health (optional). If you turn on Apple Health in onboarding or Profile, FitAgain writes finished workouts and your check-in weight to Health, and reads today's step count to show it in the app. Data read from Health stays on your device and is not sent to our servers or to any AI provider. You can turn this off at any time in Profile or in the Health app.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. If FitAgain is ever sold or merged, this policy continues to apply to your data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your photos, plans, progress and account from our servers. Anonymous accounts that have not been used for 24 months may be deleted. AI providers process requests in real time and keep them only as long as their own short safety-retention periods require.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: Profile → Delete account removes your account and data.
  • Access or correct: email us and we will send you a copy of your data or fix it.
  • Notifications: reminders are optional and can be switched off in Profile or iOS Settings.
  • Apple Health: optional, and can be switched off at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract) and, for health data and photos, with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos are stored in private storage that only your account can access.

8. Children

FitAgain is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
