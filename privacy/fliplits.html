Fliplits Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Fliplits ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Gary Gallagher. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, themes and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not ask you to create an account.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To deliver and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect information such as a device advertising identifier, device and network information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Unity's use of this information is described in the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

The app does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you have not granted tracking permission, ads are not personalized using that identifier. You can limit ad tracking at any time in your device settings.

Analytics

The app uses Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are started and finished, how long sessions last, and whether players come back. Unity Analytics may collect a device identifier, device and network information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This information is not used to identify you or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Unity's use of this information is described in the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

The app also uses GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) for the same purpose, and to receive crash reports. GameAnalytics may collect a device identifier for this app, device and network information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This information is not used to identify you or to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and the app never asks for the advertising identifier. GameAnalytics' privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple shares with the app only what is needed to confirm and restore your purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
