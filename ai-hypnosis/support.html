Support for your practice

For help with AI Hypnosis, contact northlandai@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, approximate time of the issue and a short description. Never send passwords, confirmation codes or payment-card details.

Getting started

Choose meditation or self-hypnosis and describe what you want to work on. Save and review your goals and preferences before requesting a program or session. You can edit your answers later. If generation says it is unavailable, your draft is still saved; no real script or recording has been created.

Account copies and downloads

Settings → Your account → Profile & answer sync lets you review and explicitly save or restore your profile. Account library retrieves programs and scripts already saved to that account. It does not generate new content. If an item conflicts with local content, both copies are left unchanged rather than silently replacing one.

For an account session with registered audio, open its session menu and choose Download narration. Keep the app connected until it confirms the download. Retry after a network error. Downloading does not count as listening or mark a session complete. Subscription access is required for downloading/listening in the current app flow.

Names, favorites and progress

Rename and favorite sessions from their menu. For account sessions, Account name & favorite lets you review and explicitly save/apply those labels. Original scripts and listening records stay intact. Current listening progress, check-ins and reflections remain on their original device and are not synchronized between devices.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased inside the app through your Apple account. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple account used to subscribe. To cancel an Apple subscription, follow Apple’s subscription instructions. For eligible refund requests, visit Apple’s Report a Problem service. Deleting the app or account does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy and deletion

Privacy & your data exports or erases the current local workspace. It does not erase the server account copy. Delete my account is a separate, confirmed action in your account settings. If deletion is interrupted, reopen the app and follow the retry instructions. Contact support if it cannot finish. See the privacy policy for scope and limitations.

If a session feels uncomfortable

Pause or stop. Open your eyes if you wish, notice your surroundings, and take time to feel fully alert. The player’s Take a break action opens grounding/support information. The app and support inbox are not emergency services and are not monitored continuously. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
