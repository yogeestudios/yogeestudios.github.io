Privacy policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. This policy describes AI Hypnosis, operated by Gary Gallagher (“we”). Contact us at northlandai@gmail.com about your information.

Information you choose to provide

Your profile may include a preferred name, improvement goals, motivations, obstacles, situations, personal context, and preferences for practice, narration, duration, sound and schedule. Optional answers can be left blank. Avoid including information about other people that you do not have permission to share.

The app stores session scripts, program details, favorites and names, downloaded recordings, listening position and duration, reflections, ratings, check-ins and bookings when those records exist. Listening records are not clinical measurements or proof of improvement.

Device storage and your account

A device-only space stays separate from signed-in accounts. Saving profile/answers or session labels to an account is an explicit action. Consented generation also sends the selected personal context and stores the resulting content. Opening the app does not upload the complete local history automatically. Supabase provides account authentication, database and private file storage. Your account email and sign-in requests are processed by that service. Authentication tokens are kept in the device Keychain; the app does not save your password.

Reviewing or restoring an account library downloads its saved content to the current account’s device space. Audio downloads are requested separately. Scripts and recordings stay separate. Current progress, reflections, check-ins and bookings are device-local; they are not silently merged across devices. Signing out closes the account space but does not erase its local files. Device or system backups may retain app data according to your Apple settings.

AI and narration processing

When you consent to a generation request, we send relevant profile/preferences, goal answers, program context, selected recent feedback and structured personalization to OpenAI for program or script generation. Narration requests send the saved script and selected voice to ElevenLabs. Generated plans, scripts and recordings are saved to your account. Pending requests retain their original inputs so they can be recovered after a connection loss; retrying does not substitute later edits. Unconfigured or unavailable services do not produce substitute content.

Do not include passwords, payment details or information you do not want processed. Personalization can be useful without sharing every detail of your history. OpenAI and ElevenLabs process these requests in the United States and other countries under their own API terms and may keep request data for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. We ask OpenAI not to store generation requests for later retrieval. We do not promise that providers retain nothing.

Payments and support

Apple handles App Store payments and RevenueCat manages subscription status. Your authenticated account identifier is used to associate purchase entitlements with your account. Purchase/product identifiers, subscription status and expiration are processed to verify access and enforce generation limits. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Apple. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records under their own policies and legal obligations.

If you contact support, we receive what you send. Please omit private session text unless it is necessary to explain the problem. Service providers process technical connection and security logs under their standard retention schedules. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is integrated.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which parts of the app work, the app may send anonymous usage events to our own servers, such as “app opened”, “session finished” or “recording ready”, with a few simple details like the recording length, app version and app language. These events are tied only to a random identifier created on your device. They are not linked to your account or email, never include your goals, answers, scripts or reflections, and are not used for advertising or tracking or shared with third parties. They are kept for up to 13 months. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & your data; erasing local data also starts a new random identifier.

Your controls

  • Review and edit your profile and session preferences in Settings.
  • Export local records and share locally saved recordings from Privacy & your data. These exports describe the current device, not a complete server-account export.
  • Erase local data to remove the current workspace’s records, audio and preferences. Local erasure does not delete the account copy.
  • Delete my account requests deletion of the account, associated server records and registered narration, then clears this account’s local workspace when completion is confirmed. Failed deletion can require retry; the app shows its state. Copies already downloaded on other devices, Apple backups, exports and separately retained support records may remain.
  • If your membership is canceled or expires, we keep your account, answers, check-ins, scripts and recordings so they are available if you resubscribe. Playback of personalized recordings is unavailable without an active membership. We keep this information until you delete it or delete your account; you can do either at any time, with or without a membership.
  • Deleting an account or app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage that separately through Apple.

Retention, security and rights

Local data remains until erased or removed by the device. Account data remains while the account is used unless deleted. Account data is hosted by Supabase in the United States. Provider backups and logs may keep deleted data for a limited period under Supabase’s standard schedules before it is removed. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and meet legal obligations. Access controls and encryption in transit reduce risk; no storage system is guaranteed secure.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your information, or complain to a privacy authority. Contact northlandai@gmail.com to request assistance. Identity verification may be needed to protect your account. We do not use session information for advertising.

Audience and changes

AI Hypnosis is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. This app is a wellness tool, not medical care or an emergency service. We will update this policy as the service changes and provide notice where required.
