FitAgain Support

Need help? Email yogeestudios@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, FitAgain version (Profile tab, bottom) and a short description. We usually reply within two business days.

My plan is taking a while

Building your plan from your photos usually takes 60–90 seconds. Keep the app open and connected. If it fails, tap Try again; your answers and photos are saved.

Restore my plan on a new phone

On the welcome screen, tap Sign in and use Sign in with Apple or the email you saved your account with. Your plan, photos and progress come back.

Subscriptions

FitAgain Pro is billed by Apple. Use Restore purchases on the paywall with the same Apple account. To cancel, follow Apple's subscription instructions. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Apple Health

Health sync is optional. Turn it on or off in Profile, or in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → FitAgain.

Delete my account

Profile → Delete account permanently removes your account, photos, plans and progress from our servers.

New Year Kickstart

The 2027 challenge runs January 1–31. Be active on 21 days (finish a workout or log a meal) to earn the limited badge. You have 10 spare days.
