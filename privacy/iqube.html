iQube: Intelligent Blocks: Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026

iQube: Intelligent Blocks ("the game") is made by Gary Gallagher. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device. Your progress, settings, coins, gems and purchases are saved only on your device. We do not run servers and do not collect this information.

Advertising. The game shows ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity Ads may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location, device and usage information to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads may be personalised; if you decline, they will not be. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

In-app purchases. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center to show on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact. Questions about this policy: northlandai@gmail.com
